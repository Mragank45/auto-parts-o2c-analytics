# KPI Definitions

| KPI | Definition | Formula | Target | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **DSO** (Days Sales Outstanding) | Average days to collect cash after invoicing | Open receivables ÷ credit sales of last 90 days × 90 | ≤ 45 days | Finance |
| **OTIF %** | Orders delivered on time **and** in full | Orders with goods issue ≤ requested date AND delivered qty = ordered qty ÷ total delivered orders | ≥ 95% | Logistics |
| **On-time %** | Share of orders shipped by the requested date | Goods issue date ≤ requested delivery date | ≥ 95% | Logistics |
| **In-full %** | Share of orders fully shipped | Σ delivered qty = Σ ordered qty | ≥ 98% | Warehouse / Planning |
| **O2C cycle time** | Order date to cash received | Payment date − order date (cleared invoices) | ≤ 50 days | Order management |
| **Overdue %** | Value of invoices past due | Open invoices with due date < today ÷ total open | ≤ 15% | Collections |
| **Ageing buckets** | Overdue split by age | 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+ days past due | 90+ ≈ 0 | Collections |
| **Gross margin %** | Profit on billed items | (Billed value − standard cost) ÷ billed value | ≥ 30% | Sales / Finance |
| **Accessory attach rate** | Orders containing at least one accessory | Orders with accessory ÷ total orders | ≥ 45% | Sales |
| **Discount %** | Discount given vs list price | Σ(qty × price × discount) ÷ Σ(qty × price) | ≤ 3% | Sales |
| **Blocked orders** | Orders held by a delivery block | Delivery block set and no goods issue yet | 0 older than 2 days | Order management |

RAG status in the weekly report: **Green** = meets target, **Amber** = within ~10% of target, **Red** = worse than that.
